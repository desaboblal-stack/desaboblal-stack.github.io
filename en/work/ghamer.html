Ghamer

A logo that merges the Arabic letter “غ” and the Latin G into a single geometric form, with an arrow that signals movement, growth and ambition. The design bridges Arab identity and a global character, reflecting an agency that pushes brands toward growth and tangible impact. In burgundy, beige and black, with a pattern drawn from the arrow’s shape.
