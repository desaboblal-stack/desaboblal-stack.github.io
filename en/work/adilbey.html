Adilbey Çiftliği

The identity of Adilbey Çiftliği, a farm and dairy producer. The logo combines the farmer’s face and hat with two leaves that form his moustache, expressing authenticity, a bond with the land and a natural product. The identity extends to packaging labels, flyers and business cards in dark green and gold.
