Data Pill

Visual identity for the “Data Pill” academy, which teaches data skills and gets learners hired, under the line “Learn. Build. Get Hired.” A geometric wordmark with custom-drawn letters and a clear digital character, strong enough to stand without an icon, with a D symbol drawn from it for apps. In a gradient of violet and electric blue over deep indigo, the identity extends to billboards, an event hall, social media posters, a tote bag, a mobile app and the website.
