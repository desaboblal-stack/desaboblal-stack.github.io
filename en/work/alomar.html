Al Omar Law Firm

A complete visual identity for Al Omar Law & Legal Consultancy. The logo brings together the Roman column, a symbol of law and the court, and the pans of the scales, a symbol of justice, with the word “Al” set inside a composed square. The column repeats as an ornament that runs across the printed collateral.
